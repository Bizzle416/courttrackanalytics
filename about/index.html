Years inside junior basketball.

Justin has spent years in the Victorian junior basketball system. He played representative basketball himself, then transitioned into coaching — working across both domestic and representative competitions. He later served as Coordinator for U8 and U10 domestic competitions, giving him a deep understanding of how junior clubs operate and what coaches actually need.

Then he became a parent.

Becoming a parent of a representative player changed everything. Every week, Justin watched his kids leave it all on the court — and then the game just disappeared. No data, no record of what happened, no way to track their development over a season.

The tools that existed were either built for professional environments or relied on automated systems that missed the nuance of junior basketball. So he built one.

Game intelligence built for junior basketball.

CourtTrack Analytics is a managed game-analysis service built specifically for junior basketball. Every game is watched in full, every event is logged from the footage, and the data is turned into a post-game analytics pack — delivered to coaches and families after every game.

Coaches get the full team picture. Families get their player's story. No sensors, no guesswork — just someone who understands the game, analysing it the way a coach would.
