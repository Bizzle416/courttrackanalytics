We receive your game footage after each match, watch the full replay and log every possession, shot, rebound, turnover and substitution against the game clock. That data is turned into a post-game intelligence pack — delivered to your coach and families within a few days.
Your team supplies the footage. Any camera setup works — phone, action cam, dedicated video camera. Once the game is recorded, you share a link to the footage (Google Drive, Dropbox, YouTube unlisted) through your team's submission page and we take it from there.
Every pack includes playing time and rotation breakdowns, shot charts plotted from the footage, game and season trends, and written notes on each player's performance. Coaches get the full team view; families get a personalised view focused on their player.
Each coach and team member receives a unique access code from the nominated person for each team. They enter it at courttrackanalytics.com.au/login and are taken directly to their dashboard — no app download, no account creation required.
Turnaround depends on game volume and scheduling. We'll confirm the expected timeframe when your team signs up.
We cover any junior basketball competition where teams can supply footage. Currently working with teams across Victoria, including grading and representative competitions.
Yes — we offer one free game analysis with no commitment. Submit your footage at courttrackanalytics.com.au/free-trial and we'll deliver the full pack. If you're happy, we'll talk about covering your season.
Pricing is per team per season and varies based on game volume and competition type. Visit our Pricing page or contact us at enquiries@courttrackanalytics.com.au for a quote tailored to your team.
We work with whatever footage your team can provide. As long as the court and players are visible, we can code the game. If footage is unusable for any reason, we'll let you know before starting and won't charge for that game.
