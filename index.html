How it works

Watch. Code. Deliver.

No sensors, no guesswork, no automated tagging. The accuracy comes from a person pausing the replay at every event — the same way a coach would, with the time to do it properly.

Step 1

Film & watch

Your game is filmed courtside. The analyst then watches the full replay — every quarter, every rotation, both ends of the floor.

Step 2

Code every event

Shots, rebounds, assists, steals, turnovers and playing time are logged by hand against the game clock, for your team and the opposition.

Step 3

Deliver the pack

Within days of the final siren, your coach and families get their post-game intelligence pack — stats, charts and written analysis.

The intelligence pack

What you get after every game

Playing time & rotations

Shot charts

Every attempt plotted on the court — makes, misses, and where each player's shots actually come from.

Game & season trends

Scoring runs, turnover patterns and rolling averages across recent games, filtered by competition type.

Written player analysis

Individual, human-written notes on each player's game — what the numbers show and what the replay showed. Not auto-generated.
