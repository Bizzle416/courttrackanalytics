How it works

Watch. Code. Deliver.

No sensors, no guesswork, no automated tagging. Every event is captured from full game footage and logged against the clock.

Step 1

Film & watch

Your team supplies game footage. We watch the full replay — every quarter, every rotation, both ends of the floor.

Step 2

Code every event

Shots, rebounds, assists, steals, turnovers and playing time are logged against the game clock, for your team and the opposition.

Step 3

Deliver the pack

Your coach and families get their post-game intelligence pack — stats, charts and written analysis.
